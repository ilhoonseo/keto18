Help

How do I change my carb or fasting goal?

Tap “Goal under ○g ✎” on the Food tab, or go to Settings → Daily carb goal. For fasting, use the 16 / 18 / 24-hour buttons or “⚙ Custom” on the Fast tab.

My alerts arrive late

Allow “Alarms & reminders” for Keto18 in your phone settings to get alerts right on time. The app’s Settings screen links there directly.

How do I move my records to a new phone?

In Settings, tap “Create backup file”, then “Restore from backup” on the new phone. Photos aren’t included, so save the ones you want with “Save to gallery”.

How do I delete all my data?

Use Settings → “Delete all records”, or uninstall the app / clear its storage in Android Settings.

Are the food carb values exact?

Built-in values are typical averages and vary by product. If there’s a nutrition label, use “Enter manually”.
